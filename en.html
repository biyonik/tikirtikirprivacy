Privacy policy

Tikir: Clocks & Money is an educational app that teaches children in grades 1–4 analog clocks, time, the calendar and money through play. It is developed by Ahmet Altun. This page explains what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and why none of it is ever sent to us.

In short

  • No internet permission
  • No ads
  • No personal data collected
  • No subscription

01We collect no personal data

Tikir has no server and no accounts. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or remote configuration tools. It does not request the internet permission (INTERNET), so it cannot connect to the internet by itself.

We (the developer) never receive, see, sell or share any information about you or your child.

02Data that stays on the device

To work, the app stores the following only on your device, encrypted:

  • Child profile: the name or nickname typed when the profile is created (up to 16 characters), the chosen hero (avatar), the grade and the daily goal. You can use a nickname instead of a real name.
  • Progress: completed lessons and units, stars, stickers, the streak and time spent learning.
  • Settings: language, currency, sound and read-aloud preferences, reminder time.
  • The Parent pack licence (if you bought it).

If you add a home-screen widget, a short non-personal summary such as the streak count and the next lesson is also kept on the device. None of this data is sent to us or to any other server.

App data is not backed up to your Google account or transferred to another device; it stays on this device only. So if you change phones, progress does not move to the new device. The Parent pack, however, is tied to your Google account: on the new device, sign in with the same Google account and tap “Restore purchases” in the grown-ups area to unlock it again.

03No ads

Tikir shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. The advertising ID is not used. Screens a child sees have no prices, purchase buttons, external links or social features; on a locked unit the child only sees the message “Show this unit to a grown-up”.

04Purchases and pack verification

There is no subscription. In the grown-ups area, a one-time, lifetime “Parent pack” can be bought through Google Play. Google processes your payment details; we never see them. The purchase is verified on the device with Google Play’s licence key, and the pack licence is stored encrypted on the device.

The pack is re-verified through the Google Play app on the device at least once every 3 days. For this, Tikir only uses the network state permission (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to check whether a validated internet connection exists; this permission gives no internet access. The check happens between Google Play and Google; Tikir sends no data to us.

If there is no internet for more than 3 days, the pack pauses and no progress is lost; it unlocks again by itself once the device is back online. Free content always works offline.

05Reminder notification

An optional “Daily reminder” can be turned on in the grown-ups settings. It is a local notification scheduled on the device; it uses no internet or server. When you turn it on, Android asks for the notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS). RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED is used to set the reminder again after the device restarts. You can turn the reminder off at any time.

06Report card and sharing

The Report card, unlocked with the Parent pack, is created on the device as a PDF; it shows the child’s profile name, grade and progress. It leaves the app only when you tap “Share” or “Download PDF” in the grown-ups area, and only to the app or location you choose. Tikir never sends it anywhere by itself.

07Parental gate

Purchases, settings, the progress report and external links (such as the “Enjoying Tikir?” card that opens Google Play) are behind the “Grown-ups area” gate. The gate opens with a question young children cannot easily answer.

08Android permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — the daily reminder notification.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — setting the reminder again after a restart.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — checking for a validated internet connection to verify the Parent pack (gives no internet access).
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — purchases through Google Play.

Tikir does not request internet (INTERNET), camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos and files, or advertising ID permissions.

09Deleting data

In the grown-ups area, Settings → “Reset this profile's progress” deletes all stars, stickers and the streak of that profile.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing Tikir’s storage in Android settings, deletes all Tikir data on the device (profiles, progress, settings, licence). The Parent pack is tied to your Google account; after reinstalling you can get it back with “Restore purchases”.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete.

10Children’s privacy

Tikir is designed for children in grades 1–4 and is built with the Google Play Families policy in mind. It does not collect, process or share personal information from children, so there is no data collection that would require parental consent under COPPA in the US or the GDPR in Europe.
